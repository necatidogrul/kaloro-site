Not medical advice

Kaloro estimates the nutrition of food from a photograph. Those estimates are approximations and can be wrong, which is exactly why the app shows you the assumptions it made and lets you correct them.

Kaloro is not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Talk to a doctor or a registered dietitian before changing how you eat — especially if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, are managing diabetes or another condition, take medication, or have a history of disordered eating.

Who may use Kaloro

You must be 18 or older. Kaloro will not set a daily target below 1,200 kcal for women or 1,500 kcal for men, will not plan weight loss faster than 0.75 kg per week, and will not accept a goal weight below a healthy BMI. These limits are not adjustable.

Subscription

Kaloro includes one free scan. Unlimited scanning and the correction, history and report features require a subscription.

  • Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation of purchase.
  • The subscription renews automatically unless you turn off auto-renew at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the period ends.
  • Where a free trial is offered, any unused portion is forfeited if you buy a subscription during it.
  • Manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple ID settings, or from Settings → Manage subscription inside the app.

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us. By using Kaloro you consent to us sharing information about your purchase and your use of the app with Apple when you request a refund, so that Apple can decide the request on accurate information. We do not decline refund requests on Apple's behalf.

Your content

Photographs you take stay yours. They are analysed and discarded and are never stored on our servers — see the Privacy Policy.
