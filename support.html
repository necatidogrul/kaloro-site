Support

Write to necatidogrul7@gmail.com. One person reads it, usually within a day.

The number looks wrong

It might be. Tap the cooking and portion chips on the result screen — the estimate updates as you tap. If a meal is marked in orange, we were not confident about it and would rather you checked than trusted it.

How do I cancel?

Open Settings → Manage subscription inside the app, or go to your iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep access until the current period ends. We will never make this hard.

I was charged and did not mean to be

Refunds go through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. We do not contest refund requests.

I paid but the app still asks me to subscribe

Tap Settings → Restore purchases. If that does not fix it, email us with the Apple ID email you purchased with and we will sort it out.

Does it recognise Turkish food?

Yes — that is the part we spent the most time on. Mercimek çorbası, kuru fasulye, menemen, mantı, lahmacun, pide, karnıyarık and the rest. If it misses something you eat often, tell us what it was and we will fix it.

Where do my photos go?

Nowhere. They are analysed and discarded in the same request, never stored on our servers. See the Privacy Policy.
